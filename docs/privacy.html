Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

MeetPaw is an open-source macOS menu-bar app. It runs entirely on your Mac. This policy explains what it does with your data — the short version is: it stays on your device.

What MeetPaw accesses

With your explicit consent through Google sign-in, MeetPaw uses these Google scopes:

  • Google Calendar (events) — to list your upcoming meetings so you can join them, and, if needed, to create a calendar event that carries an instant Meet link.
  • Google Meet (create meeting spaces) — to generate instant meeting links when you click “New instant meeting”.

What MeetPaw stores

Your Google access and refresh tokens are stored locally in your Mac's Keychain. Meeting and calendar data is held only in memory while the app is running and is never written to disk by MeetPaw.

What MeetPaw does not do

  • No data is sent to the developer or any third-party server. MeetPaw talks only to Google's official APIs, directly from your Mac.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of any data.

Revoking access

You can sign out in the app at any time (this deletes the stored tokens), or revoke MeetPaw's access entirely from your Google Account permissions page.
